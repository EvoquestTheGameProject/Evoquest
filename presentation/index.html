The Inspiration

We noticed what kids already love.

As students and members of today's generation, we saw that many children are naturally drawn to science-fiction games, adventure, cool animations, and immersive environments.

Sci-fi gamesAdventureCool animationsImmersive worlds

So we combined those interests with education, to make scientific learning more exciting and accessible.

The Educational System

The Tree of Science

Trunk = ScienceBranches = Fields of studyExample in our game

Science is not just one subject. It is a world of discoveries.

From branch to gameplay

Science you do, not just read.

EVOQUEST aims to introduce players to the branches of science through interactive gameplay. Chemistry and physics are the examples that show how.

Branch of scienceEducational conceptFictional gameplay activity
ChemistrySubstances, their properties, composition and reactionsFictional in-game potion-making activities
PhysicsMatter, energy, motion and forcesProblem-solving challenges that put the principles to work

Players meet scientific principles through activities and challenges, learning through gameplay.

The other six branches show the wider world of science EVOQUEST aims to introduce. They are part of the vision, not finished game features.
